# Car Service Knowledge Base

## 1. Engine / Starting Issues
- Car does not start: Ask whether the engine cranks, whether dashboard lights appear, and whether there is a clicking sound.
- If the engine does not crank and there is repeated clicking, a weak/dead battery or starter-related issue may be involved.
- If the engine cranks but does not start, possible causes include fuel delivery, ignition, immobilizer, or sensor-related issues.
- Do not advise repeated starting attempts if there is a burning smell, smoke, or unusual electrical behavior.

## 2. Battery / Electrical Issues
- Symptoms: dim lights, slow cranking, clicking during startup, electrical accessories not working, battery warning indicators.
- Basic checks: battery age, whether problem happens mainly after vehicle parked for long period.
- If smoke, burning smell, melted wiring, or visible electrical damage: advise stopping use and arranging professional assistance.

## 3. Overheating
- Symptoms: temperature warning, gauge rising, steam, coolant smell, loss of coolant.
- If overheating: advise customer to stop in safe location and switch off engine.
- Do NOT advise opening coolant reservoir/radiator cap while engine is hot.
- Professional inspection recommended before continued driving.

## 4. Brakes
- Symptoms: squealing/grinding, vibration while braking, soft/spongy pedal, pulling to one side, brake warning light.
- Grinding, very soft pedal, reduced braking performance, or brake warning light = potentially urgent.
- Advise customer to avoid driving if braking performance significantly reduced.

## 5. Tyres / Steering / Suspension
- Symptoms: vibration, pulling to one side, uneven tyre wear, steering-wheel shake, knocking sounds, vehicle sitting unevenly.
- Ask whether issue occurs during braking, acceleration, turning, or at particular speed.
- Visible tyre damage, bulge, exposed cords, or significantly deflated tyre = safety issue.

## 6. Engine Warning Light
- Ask whether check-engine light is steady or flashing; whether vehicle has reduced power, shaking, unusual noise, smoke, or stalling.
- Flashing check-engine light + severe running problems = urgent, avoid continued driving.
- Steady warning light generally requires diagnostic inspection; do not claim specific fault without diagnostic data.

## 7. Transmission / Gear Shifting
- Symptoms: delayed engagement, harsh shifting, slipping, inability to select a gear, unusual transmission noise, fluid leakage.
- Ask whether issue occurs in specific gears or when shifting from Park to Drive/Reverse.
- If vehicle cannot safely select or maintain a gear, recommend professional assistance.

## 8. Air Conditioning
- Symptoms: AC not cooling, weak airflow, unusual smell, unusual noise, intermittent cooling.
- Ask whether blower works, whether airflow is weak or normal, whether cooling changes with engine speed.
- Possible causes: refrigerant level, compressor, blower, cabin filter, electrical, airflow.
- Do not claim confirmed cause without inspection.

## 9. Unusual Noise / Smell
- Ask customer to describe sound/smell and when it occurs.
- Distinctions: squealing, grinding, knocking, rattling, hissing, burning smell, fuel smell, coolant smell.
- Burning, fuel, or strong chemical smells + smoke/fluid leakage = safety-first response.

## 10. Customer Information to Collect
- Full name, Phone number, Email (if available)
- Vehicle make and model, Vehicle year, Registration number / VIN (if required), Approximate mileage
- Primary complaint, When issue started, Constant or intermittent, Relevant warning lights
- Recent repair/service/accident, Whether vehicle currently drivable, Preferred service location or date

## 11. Safety / Escalation Rules
- Agent performs initial assessment, NOT a definitive mechanical diagnosis.
- If customer reports smoke, fire, fuel leakage, severe overheating, major brake failure, major steering failure, or immediate safety risk → prioritize safety and professional assistance.
- Never instruct customer to perform a potentially dangerous repair.
- If information insufficient → ask targeted follow-up questions instead of inventing an answer.
- Advice based on customer's reported symptoms and this knowledge base.

## 12. Example Assessment Outcome
Customer says: "The car is not starting and I hear clicking."
Agent should: collect vehicle/customer details, ask whether dashboard lights work, when issue started, whether engine cranks, whether battery recently replaced.
Suitable response: symptoms can be consistent with weak battery or starter-related issue, but proper inspection is required to confirm cause.
